Digital art / CGI rendering of a vertical code waterfall. Central bright cyan-green stream of falling Matrix-style characters, slightly wider and more luminous than the surrounding rain, cascading from top of frame down onto a dark reflective water pool with white splash and ripples at the base. The surrounding background is black with numerous thin vertical columns of green alphanumeric code raining down across the full width. The code rain is less bright on the sides, brighter in the center. Top of the waterfall is a bright white-green glow.
