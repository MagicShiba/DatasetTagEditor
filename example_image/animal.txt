Cubist oil painting, abstract geometric style, A stylized blue and yellow dog's head in the foreground, side profile, with a single large black eye and a black nose, Behind the dog, a blue and yellow human face with two eyes and a nose is layered into the composition, The background consists of various sharp-edged geometric shapes in colors of yellow, blue, red, green, black, and pink, Strong lighting creates flat color blocks with no visible single light source, The overall composition uses fragmented planes and overlapping shapes characteristic of cubism.
